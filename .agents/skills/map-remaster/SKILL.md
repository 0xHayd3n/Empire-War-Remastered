# Map remaster

**Outcome:** deliver a reversible improvement that works under an army during play, not just a spreadsheet, a material ball, or a prettier close-up. Endor is the accepted quality reference, not the default palette or vegetation for other planets. Work from the Universal checkout. Commands below run at its root.

1. **Queue.** Run `node tools/map-remaster.mjs plan --scope <scope>` (`all` for installed editions, primaries and saved orders). Read the printed report; its queue ranks distinct *pairs of winning art hashes* by map reach. Start with the largest family appropriate to the request, not the first map alphabetically. Pilot one representative **and one exception** (different sky, editor usage, mod winner, wet/dry or scale). Queue reuses research, not approvals. For space maps or mission-only maps use the [route branches](../../../docs/map-remaster-workbench.md#route-branches).
2. **Scout once.** Use [`prep --job <id>`](../../../docs/map-remaster-workbench.md#short-path-for-the-next-land-map) for one fresh mounted stock battle, its terrain/prop inventory, location worksheet and flat scan library. For a cohort use `batch --plan <report> --limit 3`. Share the same private packet with reference and material researchers; don't relaunch the map to get slot names or props. Inspect `painted-sources.png` and HUD-on squad, surface, shoreline (if present) and command shots. Read the painted area/underwater counts and actual environment, unused slots and dynamic construction slots. Record what must remain recognizable: open route, structures, pad, cover, strata, faction silhouettes and the mod's art direction. Missing winners, wrong mount or unreadable required art means **retain** until resolved. Give each material a semantic role from the image *and* its use in play. Never approve by filename, nearest color or planet name.
3. **Establish planet identity before choosing art.** Open the packet's location board; use `references --job <id> [--planet "display name"]` for a missing or new search. For each new brief, inspect at least ten distinct **place-first** internet images covering natural ground, buildings and their transition (or supported absence), with both wide and ground-height evidence. Search named landforms, settlement exteriors, architecture studies and documented filming locations; label real-world analogues as such. Count a film frame only if the place itself is the subject. Exclude cast/action, vehicle showcases, interiors, orbit and duplicate crops; native inhabitants may be incidental if they explain scale or land use. Record `subject`, `people` and a specific `settingCue` per image. If the search runs short, seek another source; never pad the board with story stills. Compare against mounted gameplay separately. For Workshop worlds prefer the mod author's specific setting. Cite what is visible, what is inference, and what you reject for **ground**, **foliage/silhouette** (including deliberate absence) and **landmark/clearing**. Save with `brief --job <id> --report <reference report> --review <decisions.json>`; [schema and missing-reference branch](../../../docs/map-remaster-workbench.md#planet-identity). If evidence is thin, search another primary source or retain that feature. A matched paint hash does not transfer a planet's art direction. Stills stay private; license production donors separately.
4. **Source all release terrain from Poly Haven.** Follow [native 2K terrain intake](../../../docs/map-remaster-workbench.md#native-2k-poly-haven-terrain). Compare candidate diffuse maps with the inspected planet views, not just sphere thumbnails. Record the reference cue, physical repeat, first-party four-channel pins and a decision for every painted and live paintable slot, including paving and markers. Plot where each would-be industrial material is painted. Limit built materials to evidenced aprons or actual construction footprints, provide a natural fallback, and check both a wilderness and a building control from the drawn terrain. A gap blocks the remaster; keep the original playable as fallback. **Audition cheaply.** For a compatible donor run `trial --job <id> --slot <n> --donor <family>`. Inspect the same army, target, light and camera original → trial → restored. Use `learn --report <report.json> --decision candidate|avoid --note "visible reason"` to remember exact source/donor pairs in version control. Read previous notes before another trial. If no donor fits (snow, lava, urban metal, alien terrain…), keep the job in research while sourcing a Poly Haven family; cook albedo/height and normal/roughness at measured scale with mips once, pin its licence/inputs/outputs. Never force the nearest Endor scan. New donors need a first-family cook/adapter; the workbench trial currently knows its eight local families.
5. **Compose the ground before binding slots.** Mark where a route wears through vegetation, shelter accumulates litter, slopes expose rock, and construction creates an intentional edge. Test those decisions against the planet views and the mounted map. Treat legacy paint as evidence, not a stencil. For each new binding, [review ground color in its authored climate](../../../docs/map-remaster-workbench.md#substrate-light-and-weather). Compare the same ground in two regions under its actual skies and weather phases; reject unmotivated hue changes without flattening real shade, exposed geology or land-use edges. Follow the [patch and blend review](../../../docs/map-remaster-workbench.md#patch-and-blend-review) under the deployed army and in a second region. For reused ground run the [two-scale variation check](../../../docs/map-remaster-workbench.md#ground-material-variation): break recurring scan marks at squad height and check for a repeated mix *within the same substrate* at command height, in both regions. Record the physical cause of variation (or referenced uniformity); an aligned apron is a control, not a defect.
6. **Account for each foliage layer.** Use [foliage evidence and zoom continuity](../../../docs/map-remaster-workbench.md#foliage-evidence-and-zoom-continuity). Derive growth forms, distribution, variation and exclusions from the inspected pictures. A missing growth form is a valid decision. Track the same roots from squad height to the mounted camera maximum and back; replace near detail with a visible distant silhouette, not an empty batch. Check the used instance range, not just the allocation or FPS.
7. **Finish the outskirts of every new or remastered land map.** Inspect the mounted board at the four camera stops and all four corners. Decide from the planet views whether to continue flat ground with its evidenced vegetation (none if barren), build procedural or licensed hills/mountains, preserve a coast, or retain an already convincing authored horizon. Join the actual terrain rim, not the camera rectangle. Keep the original camera limits, playable area, fog and simulation. Follow the [outskirts gate](../../../docs/map-remaster-workbench.md#land-map-outskirts) at maximum and low oblique angles; a straight tabletop edge anywhere blocks release.
8. **Build the biome once.** When asked for a playable remaster, do not stop at `trial`. Implement a shared visual recipe for this family and a thin binding per reviewed map. Every used or paintable terrain slot gets a complete Poly Haven material, including albedo; participating props can have a separate `replace` or `retain` reason. Pin edition, ordered playset/revision, virtual TED path/hash, winning diffuse **and** bump, every replaced model/material/sky dependency and donor hashes. Check the whole binding before changing anything. Unknown/changed sources and unsupported hardware keep authored scenery. Preserve an original switch and test disposal. Restrict placement to renderer-only geometry; never edit the TED or simulate cover, random weather phases or save state. The [diagnostic table](../../../docs/map-remaster-workbench.md#diagnose-from-the-battle) decides which systems actually need work. Extend shared renderer/cooks for a new family; do not fork a copy of Endor per map.
9. **Release gate.** [Check the first visible battlefield](../../../docs/map-remaster-workbench.md#first-visible-battlefield) on a cold Universal launch. When selected, the remaster must already be bound at reveal; original or rejected inputs must open on stock. A later `active` flag or screenshot is not launch evidence. Iterate on one risk camera before running the matrix. On a registered binding use `verify --job <id> --packet <prep/packet.json>` for fresh renderer/tier, climate, original, fallback and `fit` evidence. Otherwise run `fit --job <id> --report <composed mounted report>` for each required mounted route; register new controls rather than borrowing another planet's. Inspect the resulting boards and compare the actual squad-height silhouettes, ground transitions and command skyline with the selected references. Record a visible match and a remaining discrepancy. A contradiction reopens the recipe even when every technical check passes. Apply the [surface and border checks](../../../docs/map-remaster-workbench.md#surface-and-border-checks): isolate a visible black boundary from water, fog and baked light; verify signed donor normals against height, an edge shared by mixed cells, and actual near relief through a short camera move. Attach the `surfaceAudit` to the mounted report. Capture the *composed implementation*, not the one-slot trial. Match authored sky/weather, real deployed squads + command HUD, travel a second part of the map, and check a surface transition in motion. Add water-sheet-off, rain/clearing, lava, hardscape or foliage comparisons only where present. Apply the [Endor renderer regression controls](../../../docs/map-remaster-workbench.md#renderer-controls-from-endor) for any affected paths. Check both WebGPU and WebGL, relevant low texture tier, changed/missing inputs and exact off/on/off restoration. Verify original paint, heights, pathing, sites, buildings, fog of war, weather clock, saves/network behavior remain simulation-owned. Review timing during normal play before making performance claims. If application or rollback fails, leave original enabled; record `blocked` with the failing route/view instead of proposing a grade. Space has its own gate.
10. **Roll out by cohort.** Reuse a passing donor/cook across matching pairs, then verify each map binding in context. Parallelize independent donor research or map checks; one writer per shared recipe. Hand back playable route(s) for *released* bindings, counts of planned/trialled/accepted/retained/blocked scoped maps, reasons and next queue item. The user need not supervise every map; ask only on art-direction conflicts affecting a cohort, licensing or publishing decisions. A scanner success or pixel change is never visual acceptance.
