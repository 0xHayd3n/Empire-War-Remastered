# Universal tactical battles

**Battlefield host**: The installed game or playset that supplies the map, facilities and rules for one battle. It need not supply either army. Not the network host.

**Faction source**: The exact installed edition of stock, an overhaul, or an ordered playset from which a player draws a faction. Two factions with the same name but different sources are distinct choices.

**Detachment**: The combat units a faction can field away from its own battlefield host. Availability is specific to land or space; a detachment is not the donor's entire tech tree or campaign.

**Seat**: One participant's place in the match, with a controller, a team, and (if playing) one sourced faction. Team determines allies and opponents; era does not.
