# One realism switch for land and space

In mounted Universal, open **Settings → Video → Graphics Quality → All battles → Battle realism**. The choice takes effect during a battle; it also follows you when Universal mounts another game or mod. **Use individual settings** is the default and preserves the previous controls. **On · land and space** applies the shared treatment. **Off · land and space** bypasses it without overwriting any individual choice. Ordinary shadows, HDR, render scale, AA, other gameplay effects and the HUD remain independently controlled.

- **In orbit:** ships receive corrected paint lighting, map-sky bounce and material highlights, with separate exposure for distant dust. On the ground, units use the map’s sun, fill, sky and fog rather than the vacuum exposure; terrain uses its own multiscale textures. If sky readback is unavailable (including optional worker rendering), ground lighting falls back to the mounted sun and fills. Unlit and additive materials keep their authored treatment.
- **At close range:** the camera selects by visible screen size, not unit name, including fighters, ground vehicles, opaque animated infantry and structures. A fitted depth map follows one subject when master Shadows is on. The normal battlefield shadow remains for the rest of the scene. Material relief follows the mounted artwork. Extra geometry and masks fitted to the stock Star Destroyer still require that exact mesh; they are not copied onto unrelated models.
- **At every zoom:** current-frame sampling steadies the 3D image without accumulating history behind moving units or bolts. It covers the whole scene, so it can cost frame rate, especially on WebGL. Close and wide sample counts never stack. Both respect Standard AA, native battle scale, existing supersampling and their pixel budgets. HUD text stays at its original resolution.
- **To try it:** select a ship, squad, vehicle or building and choose **Play unit flyby** in that same section. Without a selection the camera picks a visible subject near the view. The eight-second pass can be exited early with Escape. This is a way to inspect the effects, not a prerequisite for them.

## Verification

`node tools/check-universal-battle-realism.cjs` runs through the mounted launcher in an isolated Chrome session. Set `LOOK_DOMAIN=land` for Kuat, `LOOK_VEHICLE=1` to add a stock AT-ST test fixture there, `LOOK_BACKEND=webgl` for the fallback, and `LOOK_PROFILE=1` to check that a change follows the stock → Fall of the Republic → stock round trip. Files and captures stay in the system temporary directory unless `LOOK_SHOTS` is set.

Checked the actual settings at desktop and 390px width, with keyboard navigation and reduced motion. Mounted flybys exercised non-destroyer ships at Abregado-rae, an AT-ST, and a stormtrooper. Each selected unit activated close sampling and a focused shadow. Off removed the extra sampling and focused shadow; the space frame restored exactly. On land a small residual pixel difference remained across repeated world submissions (28/761,600 pixels for the AT-ST, 76/761,600 for the WebGL infantry run), so the land frames were not byte-identical. The shared choice crossed mounted profiles while the individual space look stayed local. This is representative coverage, not a sweep of every Workshop model or a performance benchmark of the combined preset.
