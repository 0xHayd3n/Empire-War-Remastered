# Commander heraldry

In **Skirmish → Teams → Faction**, the upper crest is a still HD stencil in the exact roster’s default faction shade, including the labeled Universal markers. The rotating solid behind the representative uses the same roster shade. Its lighting and scan lines give it depth; they do not choose a second faction color. Shared artwork keeps the focused roster’s color, even when another pack supplied the design. Reduced motion holds the projection still.

**Universal faction colors.** The dot beside the group name is the exact roster’s default in-game shade. Republic & Rebellion uses **crimson**, Separatists **blue**, Empire & Imperial offshoots **green**, Underworld & syndicates **gold**, Regional powers **teal**, Unknown Regions powers **copper**, Invasion forces **magenta**, Sith traditions **violet**, and Local forces & warlords **silver**. All 75 shades are pinned by `(source profile, faction ID)`, independent of search, language and installed-pack order. The same shades color the normal campaign map, setup previews, and automatic land/space team paint. Duplicate automatic seats prefer a lighter or darker shade. Manual paint, saved battle colors, custom liveries and relative friend/enemy displays remain authoritative. Campaign units with explicit fixed markings keep them; the group shade replaces the faction-wide paint fallback. The swatch identifies the roster, not an alliance. The flat crest and projector follow the swatch, not a player’s manual team paint. The original XML and standalone game colors remain untouched.

**Browse related factions.** Open the directory icon to the left of Search to see commanders and field representatives arranged under browsing groups. Every card uses the exact roster’s crest behind a transparent bust rendered from its equipped stage figure. Choosing a card focuses that specific pack and faction within its family; choosing a group heading filters the ring to that collection. The inline crest always belongs to the exact focused roster. The group link under its title remains a shortcut. Each group tile combines distinct crests (or labeled member markers) from that group on a gradient of member colours; it is a browsing control, not a new alliance insignia. **Separatist coalition** includes the Confederacy, Commerce Guild, Trade Federation, Banking Clan and Techno Union. **Underworld & syndicates** covers Black Sun, Hutt Cartel, Pirate Bands, Criminal Underworld and Zann Consortium. **Empire & Imperial offshoots** includes the Galactic Empire, breakaway regimes such as Greater Maldrood and Eriadu Authority, Imperial warlords/defectors and the Empire of the Hand. **Sith traditions** groups the Sith Empire, Sith Warlords and Sith Cultists. These are browsing categories across eras, not declarations of alliance. Five further groups cover the other installed seats:

| Browsing group | Members |
|---|---|
| Republic & Rebellion | Galactic Republic (including aligned sector forces), Rebel Alliance and New Republic |
| Regional powers | Hapes Consortium, Mandalorian Clans, Corellia, Corporate Sector Authority and Duskhan League |
| Unknown Regions powers | Chiss Ascendancy, Rakatan Remnants and Ssi-ruuvi Imperium |
| Invasion forces | Yuuzhan Vong and Remake’s Invaders, grouped by role, not origin |
| Local forces & warlords | Independent Forces and the Clone Wars Warlords; the later Imperial warlords stay with the Imperial offshoots |

**Warlord is not one cross-era faction.** Thrawn’s Revenge distinguishes **Minor Warlords** (campaign garrisons) from **Minor Imperial Warlords** (playable campaign regimes). Only the latter now uses the shared Imperial cog in its Imperial-group green, by explicit visual override. Both Awakening entries retain **Warlord**, their singular pack name. The crest subtitle **Universal marker** identifies fallback artwork; it is not a claim about the faction's official insignia. See the [four-seat source audit](warlord-roster-audit.md) for the pictured soldiers and commanders.

Every currently installed commander has one group. Unreviewed future factions do not inherit a group from a similarly named internal key.

Requested mod artwork is read from the installed donor’s private cache. If the selected visual donor is not installed, the roster retains its available native art or neutral standard. Supplied reference designs are shared presentation assets. No Workshop directory or manifest is modified. Original faction labels remain searchable; assigning a commander always commits the original `(pack, faction)` roster, never a visual donor.
