# Marooned on Geonosis

[Play the remaster](http://127.0.0.1:8590/#geonosis-remaster) · [Play the original](http://127.0.0.1:8590/#geonosis-original). Stock *Forces of Corruption*, `_MP_LAND_GEONOSIS.TED`. The two links are session-only profiles; **Geonosis badlands** is also available in Graphics Quality.

Follow the ground from the western infantry to the spire at (1279, 4838), then compare the eastern wash and steam fissure. Most of the floor is fine sediment over harder red-brown crust. Gravel fans gather at changes of grade and in scoured washes. The paler mineral trace is dry. Steep faces expose mottled stone; the widely painted `NB_Geonosis_tower` image is **not** a permit to lay paving across a rock wall. All 21 spires, 681 rock clusters, 16 larger rock forms and 23 steam/crack placements stay where the map put them. The remaster adds no foliage, structures or liquid. No change to the TED, camera limits, original heights, pathing or sites.

The [Geonosis establishing view and arena court](https://www.starwars.com/databank/geonosis) and [crevasse-bridge concept](https://www.starwars.com/series/clone-wars/weapons-factory-concept-art-gallery) establish the ground-to-hive relationship. Badlands, Bisti, Kasha-Katuwe and Golden Canyon photographs are Earth analogues for erosion, not architecture or a color target. The terrain fits the open ground around authored spires; it does not reconstruct the much denser skyline in the concept art.

**What the audition changed.** A thinly bedded `cliff_side` scan looked convincing in isolation. The western scarp in mounted gameplay showed crosswise hatching from the rotated bedding. Holding all diffuse RGB constant removed it; holding just the normals or the vertex shade constant did not. A one-family control reproduced the parquet on that shelf. The release uses a non-directional rock on the shelf and a matched projection on the two steep aspects. A speckled 4 m floor scan also proved too conspicuous as blanket cover. It is now restricted to fans. No corrective scene grade or terrain blur.

**Verification.** The checker draws all 31 used/dynamic slots with six authenticated native 2K materials, reads the two regional within-substrate fields, and probes the real `Geo_CLear`, `Sunrise_Clear` and `Noon_Clear` skies. None authors a weather scenario. A survey of all 96,721 natural vertices found no paving at launch; the construction-only slot is not evidence of a live build event. The exterior joins 1,240 original rim vertices with 25,552 renderer-only triangles, broken rock benches and clear passes, no crowns or stands. The camera can still reach 1600. Testing is for this exact board, not Workshop variants, sustained performance, or a mission conversion.
