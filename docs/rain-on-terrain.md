# Rain on terrain

In a rainy Endor land battle, watch a path beside the trees: exposed soil darkens without turning glossy. Paving gains more sheen at a low angle. The ground doesn't snap back when the rain stops, and a walker on damp ground kicks up fewer dust puffs. Enable **Settings → Video → Graphics Quality → World environments → Rain on terrain**, or **Battle realism → On**. The individual default is off. The switch affects the ground and its dust, not whether the map has rain.

## What changes

- A renderer-local saturation value follows liquid weather phase strength, including transitions. An ash storm with `rain` in its emitter filename is not rain. At full rain the ground fills exponentially (13-second time constant); a fully soaked surface takes 70 clear battle-seconds to dry. Pause freezes the response. The current weather is tracked even when the setting is off; switching it off immediately shows the original material. No save format changes; a newly loaded scene starts dry.
- The existing splat weights blend the response of each painted terrain layer. The TED surface kind and, for unclassified layers, texture name determine absorption and sheen. Unknown materials get mild darkening and very little sheen. The film uses the existing bump, view angle and map fill; steep slopes retain less film and submerged terrain is excluded. No reflection texture, new terrain mesh, puddles, local shelter detection or extra render pass. A building roof or tree isn't an occlusion mask for rain.
- A per-mover fractional counter thins recognizable dust/foot-puff **particle bursts** from surface animation events. It doesn't gate surface lookups, track placement, footprints, sounds, mod-authored scorch marks or combat FX. Splash and snow puffs are not dust. It doesn't add dust to infantry or to assets that had none, or change movement, weapon accuracy, existing weather rules, visibility or simulation RNG.

## Verification

`npm run test:ground-rain` checks phase easing, timestep invariance, complete drying, material classes, particle selection and master overrides. `node tools/check-ground-rain.cjs` opens mounted Universal at `http://127.0.0.1:8590/`, uses the real setting, and starts a land Instant Battle. Repeat with `LOOK_BACKEND=webgl`. `LOOK_WORLD=Tatooine` selects the dry control; `LOOK_WORLD=Kuat LOOK_PROFILE=1125571106` selects the installed mod station. Playwright must be on the Node module path. Local reports/captures are written under the OS temporary directory `universal-ground-rain`; no mounted textures or captures are distributed.

- **Stock Endor, 1920×1080, both backends:** one strategy camera pose and army, clear, 37 seconds of full rain, then eight seconds clear. The fixture uses actual `RAIN_RANDOM` phases and advances particle time/ground wetness between poses rather than fast-forwarding units or combat. Live frame integration is also checked before freezing the scene. The emitter starts/stops; saturation reads 0 → 0.942 → 0.828. A matched on/off pair at each phase separates the ground from the existing rain/fog. After full drying and on switching off there were no pixels with a luminance difference greater than 3/255 in the reset comparisons.
- **Dust:** replaying 48 identical mounted footfall events at the same spot requested **48 dry vs 9 wet particle bursts**, with **48 decal and 48 sound calls** in both. This counts the presentation decisions rather than guessing the number of individual animated dust sprites on screen.
- **Dry Tatooine:** zero wetness and no pixels over the comparison threshold after 60 seconds of non-liquid weather. **Kuat:** this station has no rain scenario; the comparison deliberately supplies mounted `RAIN_CONSTANT`, never enabled automatically in the game. With classification hints removed from a mounted layer in an isolated material test, grazing luminance changed 129 → 112/255, with only ~1 level from sheen on both backends. The same known paving had ~26 levels from sheen at grazing incidence, ~2 near upright.
- **Warm render-pan frame intervals, off → on (median / p95, ms):** on Endor, WebGPU 5.6/11.1 → 5.6/11.1; WebGL 5.6/5.6 → 5.6/5.6. Kuat WebGPU was ~5.6/11.2 in either mode. Kuat WebGL was variable: one comparison was 5.6/11.1 → 11.1/16.7, and a slower repeat was 27.6/38.8 → 22.2/33.3. These are short 1080p pans at fixed simulation time, not sustained combat, mobile hardware or an independently measured offscreen-worker renderer.
