# Universal settings inventory

This is a first pass over settings with an identifiable owner in the engine or the installed campaign atlas. It is not a promise that arbitrary Workshop scripts export a settings API. Universal carries changes made in System Settings (graphics, audio, bindings, camera, HUD and other portable player preferences) into the next mounted game before it starts. **Back** and **Esc** keep changes; no separate Apply is needed. Each profile retains its other values. Saves, map and fleet drafts, installed language, texture packs, network identity and relay settings do not travel with these preferences. It does not edit Workshop files or rewrite existing saves. Controls labelled **current campaign** change that running campaign and are included the next time you save.

## Configurable solo Battlefront rules

Use **Gameplay → General → New solo Battlefront skirmishes** for profile defaults. On the **Skirmish → Settings** board, select a land map with a player seat and check **Play this match in Battlefront mode** to override individual rules. **Use my defaults for this match** drops only the board overrides. The [setup](../public/js/game/skirmishsetup.js) snapshots validated choices on launch; opening Settings during a battle changes future defaults, not the active rules. Campaigns, multiplayer (including Battlefront hosts), story missions, spectators, Instant Battle, benchmarks, and restored sessions don't read these defaults.

| Rule | Choices | Standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tickets lost per infantry / vehicle casualty | Infantry: 1, 2 or 3. Vehicle: 1, 2, 3 or 5. | 1 / 2, charged once to the team's existing bank. Starting tickets remain separate. |
| Holding a majority of unlocked points | Drain 1 opposing ticket every 2, 5, 10 or 20 s, or switch the drain off. | 5 s. No drain on a tie. |
| Time to neutralize / capture a command post | Independently use 0.5×, 1×, 1.5× or 2× the map time for each phase. | 1× / 1×. Applies to reinforcement posts only, not build pads. [Land capture logic](../public/js/game/land.js) still decides who can capture, honors the opening lockout, and unwinds an interrupted phase at its own rate. |
| Player redeployment delay | 3, 5, 8 or 12 s after death. | 5 s. Does not shorten a shuttle flight or override an unavailable spawn. |
| Bot reinforcement delay | Half, standard, 1.5× or double time. | 10 s, shortened to 4 s when behind on reinforcement posts. Both timers scale together; caps and exhausted tickets still apply. |
| Squad deployment points | Off, or offer up to 1, 2 or 4 friendly squads. | Up to 4. Limits mobile deployment choices, not team size, command posts, home bases, or the ability to watch a teammate. |
| Squad deployment wait after leader is hit | 3, 5, 8 or 12 s. | 5 s. The leader must also be out of active combat and available. Doesn't change the player's death timer or the bot reinforcement queue. |
| First shuttle wave delay | 1, 3, 6 or 10 s from the start of the shuttle clock. | 3 s. Both sides share the schedule; boarding doesn't restart it. |
| Time between shuttle waves | 10, 15, 20 or 30 s between waves, not between individual craft. | 15 s. An empty wave sends nothing. Flight time and the stagger between craft in a wave stay unchanged. |
| Infantry per shuttle | 4, 6 or 8 infantry passengers. | 8. Overflow boards additional craft in the wave; vehicles keep their own transport. These three rules do not enable Shuttle deploys. |
| Points per hero deployment | 500, 1,000, 1,500, 2,000 or 3,000. | 1,500 for the first hero and each subsequent unlock. Doesn't grant a hero the faction lacks. |
| Infantry healing outside a friendly post or base | Off, 2.5%, 5% or 10% of maximum health per second after five seconds without a hit. | 5%. Friendly healing zones and the existing hero exception are unchanged. |

`tools/test-battlefront-rules.mjs` checks defaults, validation, isolated snapshots and excluded launch modes. `tools/check-universal-battlefront-rules.cjs` launches a mounted Kuat skirmish from the board and exercises real casualty debits, majority drain, redeployment timers, hero thresholds and infantry healing. Its [objective check](../tools/check-battlefront-objectives.cjs) takes and neutralizes a real post at different rates, tests interrupted progress and checks that a recently hit squad leader becomes selectable again after the chosen wait. Run it with `--shuttles` to check both wave clocks, the occupied cabin display and overflow into additional transports. The [shuttle tests](../tools/test-shooter-shuttle.mjs) cover empty waves, separate vehicles and both reduced capacities.

## Verification

From the Universal checkout, with the mounted host running, `node tools/check-universal-settings.cjs` tests the title route, wheel scrolling and fixed Back at desktop/phone sizes, bindings, preference writes and reload. With a declared installment-payment source it also opens the stock Galactic Conquest board and checks that the saved rule is selected and can be reset. It follows Settings choices into the skirmish board, fleet editor, fleet registry, and matchmaking console. Set `SWR_TEST_SETTINGS_PAUSE=1` to launch the campaign and check that changing the live map overlay from pause leaves the new-campaign preference alone. The test uses a fresh browser context. `tools/test-universal-atlas.mjs` covers launch validation; `tools/test-shooter-view.mjs` and `tools/test-fleet-lod.mjs` cover the respective runtime paths. `tools/check-universal-combat-feedback.cjs` launches a mounted Kuat land skirmish and inspects its actual sight, feed and lighting. `tools/test-radar-contacts.mjs` exercises land and space contact rendering with fogged, cloaked and allied units. This audit did not play every pack, compare every graphics setting pixel by pixel, or listen with a desktop screen reader.
