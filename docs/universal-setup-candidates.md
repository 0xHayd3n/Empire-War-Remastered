# Which mod options belong in campaign setup?

Source audit against Universal `081d7e7`, stock Forces of Corruption and the six locally installed overhauls. This note records the original candidates at that revision. The Fall custom layout and human production payment recommendations have since shipped; see [the setup guide](universal-library.md) for their current behavior. The distinctions are important: selecting a real campaign earlier, requesting a native action from the selected mod, and implementing a new optional Universal rule have different effects. No campaign was launched for this audit.

## Move a real choice earlier

| Candidate | What the mod actually does | What to move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fall of the Republic: Custom GC layout | `GCCampaignLoaderCustom.lua` asks for FTGU layout, Small, Medium, Large or Influencers after loading. It maps the choice and human faction to a campaign, then forwards the faction index and difficulty to `Load_Another_GC`. For example, Small + Galactic Republic resolves to `Sandbox_Custom_Small_Empire`; the XML destination exists but is deliberately unlisted. | **Best first change.** Offer only the choices the reviewed loader can reach, as maps/variants. Go straight to the same destination. This does **not** decide where the player places their starter, which units they pick, or how the destination script distributes them. Keep those interactions native. |
| Thrawn’s Revenge: optional rosters | The native **Enable Canon Units** action unlocks the MC75 and Starhawk for the New Republic and signals its admiral system. **Fractalsponge Units** unlocks a different list for the Empire and Greater Maldrood. These are unlock actions, not symmetric on/off switches. | Offer “use campaign default” or “request unlock at start” on eligible starts. Do not spawn ships, bypass production prerequisites, or grant them to another faction. Verify actual build and command-staff options. |
| Fall and Revan: appearance | Fall has clone legion colors, Republic fleet markings, B1 and CIS paint controls. Revan has a seven-color lightsaber cycle; the visual script reads it in campaign land battles, not on the galactic map. Era/story scripts can set it again. | An **initial** choice for the relevant faction, not a permanent override. Apply after native initialization; verify an affected unit in the relevant battle. Keep later story changes and the in-game controls. |
| Thrawn, Fall and Revan: Cruel AI / factional fighters | The respective handlers switch AI profiles or toggle which faction’s fighters a supported carrier draws from. In the fighter resolver the flag uses the carrier’s native faction rather than its current owner. Cruel AI has an initialization guard and changes nonhuman AI assignments; it is not the normal difficulty selector. | Good pack-specific candidates, not universal rules. Prove a changed AI assignment or fighter complement, not just a flag. Honor the native action’s availability, cost, and ordering. |

## Offer a rule in another mod

These three candidates already have consumers in the browser engine. Values below are from the winning `Data/XML/GameConstants.xml` read through [the pack resolver](../tools/megfs.mjs), once per profile. At the audited revision none was allowlisted as a cross-mod setup override.

| Proposed setup label | Installed evidence | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Human production payment: upfront / as construction progresses** | `Pay_As_You_Go` is true in Remake (`1770851727`), false in stock and the other five overhauls. | **Best first rule.** Under installments the player can queue without the full price; construction waits when the next payment is unaffordable. The engine already distinguishes these cases and handles cancel/refund in [galacticproduction.js](../public/js/game/galacticproduction.js). Keep AI budgeting separate. |
| **Income interval: 40 / 45 simulation seconds** | `Fiscal_Cycle_Time_In_Secs` is 40 for Thrawn, Fall and Revan, 45 for stock, both Awakening mods and Remake. | Changes when income arrives. Route the per-campaign value through payments, AI budgeting, the HUD, and direct readers such as credit siphon duration; don’t just change the countdown. See [economy](../public/js/game/galacticeconomy.js), [AI](../public/js/game/galacticai.js), [HUD](../public/js/game/galactichud.js), [corruption](../public/js/game/galacticcorruption.js) and [log data](../public/js/game/galacticlogdata.js). |
| **Base production time: 80% / 100%** | `Production_Speed_Factor` is 0.8 in Awakening of the Rebellion (`1397421866`) and Awakening of the Clone Wars (`3387971722`), 1.0 elsewhere. | It multiplies **time**, not output or price. Account for [player queues](../public/js/game/galacticproduction.js), [AI estimates](../public/js/game/galacticai.js) and [forces](../public/js/game/galacticforces.js); retain existing discounts and rounding. |

For a more distinctive new mechanic, Awakening of the Clone Wars’ Confederacy **bounty-hunter contracts** are a useful model: the Big Full Confederacy player plot registers `Scripts/Story/Story_Bounty_Hunters_Confederacy.lua`, which tracks recruitment, contract duration and cooldown for named hunters. A portable version would be an original mission using only the receiving pack’s eligible units, excluding story-critical heroes, with saved expiry/return rules. Don’t import its hunters. Likewise the EAWX **blockade attrition** idea depends on actual planetary defenses, eligible orbiting ships and exemptions. Thrawn’s `Scripts/Library/eawx-plugins/blockade-attrition/BlockadeAttrition.lua` expressly requires permission for code reuse. Neither is a plug-in switch.

## Keep the defaults honest

- Already in setup: difficulty; credits, tech and auto-resolve where the selected row permits them; reviewed dates/regimes and the supported Thrawn FTGU progression choice. Treasury caps, political-control timing and the original **Star Map Expedition** adaptation already have campaign-local paths. See [the setup guide](universal-library.md), [rule catalog](../tools/universal-rule-catalog.mjs), [selector table](../tools/universal-loader-table.mjs) and [launch validator](../tools/universal-atlas.mjs).
- Don’t present effects as choices: on supported Awakening maps, starting-force scripts already use **difficulty** to choose a faction/planet force list (`Scripts/Story/Story_Sandbox_DifficultyGCSpawner.lua` and `Story_Sandbox_Difficulty_Starting_Forces.lua`). Explain that consequence where applicable rather than adding an independent garrison toggle. Nor is the hidden, disabled `IDC_ALLOW_RANDOM_EVENTS` in [campaignmenu.js](../public/js/game/campaignmenu.js) evidence for a working random-events switch.
- A native choice belongs to its exact mod revision, campaign, faction and variant. If the native path cannot be validated, leave the original selector. For a portable rule, keep **Campaign default** as no override, validate the donor and value server-side (including explicit `false` for payment), and save the resolved value with this campaign. Do not mutate shared constants, execute another pack’s scripts, or mount anything just to browse.
- Acceptance checks: for Fall compare direct vs native destination, faction, difficulty and first playable state, including remaining setup prompts. For payment compare debit, insufficient-funds pause, refund and resume on a partially paid order. For both, check a subsequent default start is unchanged.
