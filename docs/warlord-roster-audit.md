# Warlord names, marks and representatives in the commander browser

Compared the installed packs against the mounted Universal atlas (`facts` labels, `commanders`, `stage` and `emblems`). The four entries the ring collects under the word **Warlords** are not four versions of one polity. In particular, the family text in the rail is not the localized faction name; `c.leader` is searchable even when the figure has a different, accurate `c.name`. Paths below are relative to each pack’s `Data/`. This audit records why the selector distinguishes the two Thrawn seats by campaign role, calls both Awakening entries **Warlord** (singular), and identifies non-native crests as **Universal marker**. It does not change campaign ownership or skirmish rosters.

| Installed pack / seat | Name and artwork | Who the figure represents |
|---|---|---|
| Thrawn’s Revenge / `Warlords` | **Minor Warlords**. This is the non-player campaign holder. Its faction definition names an infantry icon but no multiplayer faction emblem. The current ring therefore draws a Universal marker, not an authenticated logo for that holder. | **Phase II Dark Trooper** is a real unit: `Units/Imperial_Infantry_Companies.xml` assigns its company to `Warlords`. It is not a named commander. The former **Duron Veertag** search association was removed: `Duron_Veertag_Team` is `Rebel` (New Republic). Harrsk, by contrast, is one documented campaign commander, not the head of all minor warlords. |
| Thrawn’s Revenge / `Imperial_Proteus` | Also localized **Minor Warlords**. This is the playable campaign choice of minor regimes. The menu calls it **Minor Imperial Warlords**. Its verified native crest is `I_BUTTON_FACTION_WARLORD.TGA`, with `P_ALLIED_WARLORD.TGA` as a higher-detail source. The current exhibition explicitly overrides those with the shared Imperial cog, painted in this seat’s Imperial-group shade. | **Vedder** is an eligible field commander: both his actor and his company explicitly list `Imperial_Proteus`. He is not its overall leader. **Bliss Shargael** is the leader of the *I-sector* start and uses an AT-TE in this pack; he is not Vedder or the leader of every selectable start. |
| Awakening of the Rebellion / `Warlords` | The localized label is **Warlord** (singular). Its faction definition points to generic pirate icons and has no dedicated multiplayer crest. The current ring shows a Universal marker. | **Blitzer Harrsk** is a verified warlord: the campaign places him on Mustafar under this exact owner. **Stormtrooper** is legitimate as an on-foot representative, not as Harrsk: the same faction starts with stormtrooper squads on Kessel. |
| Awakening of the Clone Wars / `Warlords` | Also **Warlord** (singular), with generic pirate icon references rather than a distinct faction crest in its definition. The current Universal marker is not native heraldry. | **Santhe Security Sergeant**, present in the roster of the Warlords-affiliated Santhe Security Platoon. He replaces the Phase I clone rifleman, whose effective actor affiliation is `Neutral`; sharing an AT-PT with Republic forces was not a link to that infantryman. This is a squad-level link and not evidence that the individual leads the faction. |

### Sources and checks

- **Thrawn’s Revenge** (`1125571106`): `XML/Factions.xml:9059–9088,9090–9139` distinguishes the two exact owners, playability and preview icon. `Text/MasterTextFile_ENGLISH.txt` maps `TEXT_FACTION_WARLORDS` to **Minor Warlords**. `Scripts/Story/GCMenu_FactionText.lua:148–149` and `Scripts/Library/ProteusWarlordLibrary.lua` (header and `ISECTOR` entry) explain the holder/playable-start distinction. The mod also uses the Warlord graphic for the *Minor Warlords selection button* (`XML/CommandBarComponents_TR.xml:3274,3283`; `TEXT_TOOLTIP_FACTION_SELECTION_17`). That supports using it as a **shared family mark** if explicitly labeled, but does not establish a distinct badge for the holder. `XML/Heroes/New_Republic_Heroes_Army.xml`, `XML/Heroes/Legitimacy_Group_Heroes.xml`, `XML/Heroes/Empire_Heroes.xml`, and `XML/Units/Imperial_Infantry_Companies.xml` contain the named actor/company definitions. `XML/Conquests/Historical/11_ABY_Reunification/Campaigns_Reunification.xml:1018` assigns `Harrsk_Shockwave` to `Warlords` at Kalist.
- **Awakening of the Rebellion** (`1397421866`): `XML/FACTIONS.XML` (`Warlords`); `Text/TranslationManifest.xml` (`TEXT_FACTION_WARLORDS`); `XML/Conquests/Canon/Conquests_Canon.XML:7071,7145`; `XML/GroundCompaniesEmpire.xml` (`Imperial_Stormtrooper_Squad_Dummy` includes `Stormtrooper`); `XML/UNITS_HERO_WARLORDS.XML` (`Warlord_Harrsk` is a space actor).
- **Awakening of the Clone Wars** (`3387971722`): `XML/FACTIONS_CW.XML` (`Warlords`), `Text/TranslationManifest.xml` (`TEXT_FACTION_WARLORDS`); `XML/Gameobjectfiles.xml:196,212` loads both the clone and Santhe definitions. In `XML/UNITS/GROUND/INFANTRY/Units_Ground_Infantry_Santhetrooper.XML` see `Santhetrooper_Sergeant` (272), `Neutral_Santhetrooper_Squad` (504; affiliation 524), and its tactical composition (550–563). `Scripts/LIBRARY/libDefenseGarrisonTable.lua:843–844` pairs the squad with its garrison composition. The mounted game database independently resolves the squad to **Santhe Security Platoon**, affiliation `Warlords`, and the clone rifleman to affiliation `Neutral`. On the mounted Teams ring the sergeant uses `NI_SANTHETROOPER.ALO`, its bound idle and the actor’s authored `Weap_SG82.ALO` on `B_Gun`.
- The **Sith Warlords** in Revan’s Revenge and **Imperial Defectors** in Remake are separate display identities, not additional instances of these Minor Warlords. An internal ID or shared Universal marker is not a reason to merge them.
