# Yavin 4 — reference-led jungle revision

[Play the jungle revision](http://127.0.0.1:8590/#yavin-remaster) · [Play mounted originals](http://127.0.0.1:8590/#yavin-original). Both launch stock FoC Yavin Training Grounds. For the same troops, weather and light, stay in one battle and toggle **Yavin 4 jungle** in Graphics Quality. The session links do not overwrite edition or graphics preferences.

## Why the first pass was rejected

The old pilot passed rollback but retained Endor evergreen silhouettes. The [Yavin history still](https://www.starwars.com/yavin-4-history-gallery), [Massassi outpost](https://www.starwars.com/databank/base-one) and [Andor production-design stills and concept art](https://www.starwars.com/news/andor-season-2-world-building-production-design-feature) show a different relationship: overlapping spreading crowns flank the temple; tall irregular trunks and broad leaves frame people; the work apron stays clear. Interior, space and ceremony images from the search do **not** answer what to plant. The [inspected brief](../tools/remaster/directions/d96aa07683839825c24b8653663e554a88948f6d4e2ff1a198d77b426f1bb03e.json) distinguishes observation from interpretation. Photographs are private reference material, never input to a runtime texture.

## Changes in play

- Nine exact authored model classes (1,680 tree, cluster, canopy and ground-cover placements) now provide positions for broadleaf trees and jungle understory in place of conifers and temperate weed clumps. A near/far volumetric cutout of Poly Haven's Jacaranda supplies the branching canopy; three Anthurium variants grow beside ferns. The rendering uses 1,588 tree placements across the map, **not** 1,588 near-detail trees per frame. No TED objects are moved, deleted or made into colliders. The original Massassi temples, ruins, rock outcrops, observation towers, routes and build sites remain.
- All 18 terrain draw slots, including dynamic construction and the single good-ground sample, use nine native 2K Poly Haven PBR sets. The original atlas is **not bound** while the remaster is active. Laid stone keeps straight joints; exposed greenery, under-crown litter, the sandy approach and dark earth use separate scans. See the [slot decisions and reference citations](../tools/remaster/terrain/d96aa07683839825c24b8653663e554a88948f6d4e2ff1a198d77b426f1bb03e.json), [terrain assets and build](../public/art/yavin-ground/SOURCES.md), and [plant credits](../public/art/yavin-jungle/SOURCES.md). The references establish the relationship of jungle, apron and temple, not the identity of a particular scan. The temple building itself is not terrain.
- The switch authenticates the FoC TED, ground pairs, affected mounted ALOs and their texture dependencies, replacement packs and target counts before applying anything. Failure retains the entire original scene. Ground relief, draw slots and planting are renderer-only. Weather/fog of war, terrain heights, passability and gameplay remain map-owned.

## Foliage through command zoom

Previously the camera-distance budget removed all broadleaf herbs at 430 and all procedural ferns at 680. The same grounded plants now transition between the detailed mesh and an eight-triangle [cutout of the actual plant](../public/art/yavin-groundcover/SOURCES.md), including its silhouette from above. This retains Yavin's existing fern and the three Anthurium shapes without a new species or a second set of root positions. Canopy trees keep their own near/far crowns. Foreground readability uses the shared foliage fade, not a missing tree; normal offscreen culling and script visibility still apply.

The mounted audit follows ferns in the deployed clearing, broadleaf plants on the jungle slope, and trees at the treeline. It checks exact root positions across both earlier cutoffs, the canopy handoff, command zoom, distance 1600 (the mounted maximum), and back. Class-on/off frames verify a visible contribution. The static planting plan is not regenerated on zoom. Streams grow from live demand and update only within the active matrix range. These are coverage and submission checks, not a battle FPS benchmark.

## Where paving belongs

The same stock tile-rock paint has 2,696 vertices spread both around the Massassi temples and out in the field. It is not a land-use map. The remaster now uses aligned slabs only where that paint overlaps one of two reviewed temple thresholds; elsewhere it draws the natural substrate. Mossy rock is concentrated at outcrops and exposed slopes, not stamped wherever the editor used tile-rock. The isolated pale marker uses natural ground. Concrete requires a real construction-paint receipt, not a radius around a prop. On the mounted starting map, 35 vertices used laid slabs and 2,661 were routed back to natural ground. No construction concrete was present in that initial sample. The jungle, temple contact and same-slot wilderness control are all in the mounted review.

## The army clearing

The previous Poly Haven pass still reproduced the editor's brushwork: pale grit, dark earth and gray rock changed abruptly beneath the squads. The new layout follows two connected muster tracks, canopy shelter, damp pockets and outcrops. Broad patches of bare soil can meet green cover without a square of rock between them. The two tracks are an interpretation of this training ground, not a claim about the film set. Only rendering reads these masks; they do not instruct the army where to walk. Both backends and the close displaced mesh use the same packed cover, registered scan heights and separate built-ground permission. No scan was recolored, replaced or blurred to make the transition.

In the 33×27-vertex army control, **704 of 1,722** neighboring pairs changed dominant family with the previous layout; **270** do now. Vertices in isolated patches of four or fewer fell from **14.1% to 2.4%**. The separate southern control went from **718 to 299** changes across 1,897 pairs. The private `planet-fit.html` boards compare the previous layout and the new one in the same battle. From command zoom the jungle now borders a worn clearing; at boot height the grit is visible. The open centers are still broader and barer than the forest-floor reference, intentionally keeping the training map's deployed armies and approaches readable.

## Forest edge and surface mapping

The apparent black water at the Yavin treeline was on **fully visible dry terrain**. The map's baked vertex shade reached zero; both renderer paths multiplied it into the finished surface, after dynamic sunlight and fill. It now bounds indirect light rather than blanking the lit ground. Fog-of-war sampling and unit visibility are unchanged. A neighboring cell with retained material used to switch the entire quad to a different blend; its far corner could leave a line even at a shared edge. The mixed shader now uses continuous retained coverage, per-donor height ranking and linear-light mixing. The local displaced mesh follows that same coverage and ranking. Both jungle scans had the green normal channel facing the wrong direction for top-down texture rows; the corrected production DDS maps agree with signed height slopes on both axes. Close geometry and material normal/parallax detail are different scales; command zoom does not tessellate the whole map.

## Review

On the real HUD-on battlefield the stepped temple now rises above irregular rounded crowns, rather than a grid of needle tiers. The forest edge has forked trunks and broad leaves, while deployed troops remain readable in the clearing. The training map's open lanes are wider than the forest interior in the production design, and close crowns still use cutouts; this is not a recreation of a film set. We kept the tactical layout rather than filling those lanes to imitate a still.

### Beyond the camera limits

The original 218×218 terrain stops at 4,340 world units; the camera pivot still stops at `[400,400]` and `[3960,3960]`. At the northwest command stop the sky used to show around a triangular map corner. A render-only annulus now follows all 868 original edge vertices, including their heights and submitted normals. It carries the reviewed ground scans into irregular wooded foothills with saddles. Trees along the join reuse the field’s actual depth-sliced Jacaranda; distant stands use side and overhead projections of that crown. The middle ridges hide the far edge. No camera, TED, construction, collision, pathing or fog change.

`YAVIN_OUTSKIRTS_ONLY=1 node tools/check-yavin-remaster.cjs` sweeps four sides and four corners, then tries maximum distance at shallow pitch and removes the outermost mesh strip as a skyline control. In the matched northwest WebGPU command window, exposed backdrop pixels went from 392,413 to zero; stock rollback changed zero sampled pixels. WebGL and rainy 512px WebGL also passed. This is a Yavin binding, not a default mountain or planting policy for other worlds.

`tools/remaster/bind-yavin.mjs` builds the pinned binding from the scoped job and reviewed direction. `tools/test-yavin-recipe.mjs` checks material and plant hashes. `tools/check-yavin-remaster.cjs` checks original/remastered/restored at the deployed squad, command zoom, a jungle slope, treeline, temple approach, stone and temple. Also exercise WebGL, 512px, authored rain, original link and corrupt crown fallback via `LOOK_BACKEND=webgl`, `YAVIN_LOW=1`, `YAVIN_RAIN=1`, `YAVIN_ORIGINAL=1`, `YAVIN_FAIL=1`. `map-remaster.mjs fit --job <id> --report <mounted report>` pairs captures with the inspected references.
