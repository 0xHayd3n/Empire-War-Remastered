# Endor vegetation materials

`node tools/endor-plants-assets.mjs --verify` checks the native bark and alpha-cutout atlas. The cook script lists the pinned first-party inputs for [Brown Bark 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_01), [Fern 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02), [Nettle Plant](https://polyhaven.com/a/nettle_plant), [Grass Medium 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_02) and [Pine Tree 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_tree_01). All are [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). New wooden trunks and branches use a separate opaque bark material; leaf alpha never cuts holes in a trunk. These are materials from the assets, not a copy of Poly Haven's mature pine geometry. No stock textures are packed here. `bark-normal.png` packs the original tangent normal in RGB and the same CC0 Brown Bark 01 scan’s **roughness** in A (1K Rough PNG, md5 `01853f420746ad757a3add59bcd1a9e4`). It repeats in both directions alongside albedo. This alpha is not a cutout or stock gloss.
