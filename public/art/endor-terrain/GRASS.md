# Meadow grass

The seven-family cook filled the grass slot with Poly Haven **Leafy Grass**, which measures
tanner and browner than the meadow it stands in for: its albedo averages 151, 131, 89 sRGB
(red ahead of green by 20 levels) and only 16% of its pixels are green-dominant. The replacement
is the ambientCG photogrammetry scan **Ground 037**, the same scan geometry and lighting, and its
own colours: albedo averages 152, 147, 88 (green up 14 levels on red, 20% green-dominant). Nothing
is tinted, graded, upscaled or painted — the difference is the donor's own scan.

`node tools/cook-endor-grass.mjs` cooks only this one family into the same packed pair the other
seven use, and writes the `grass` row for `materials.json`.

| Asset | [Ground 037](https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground037), ambientCG (Struffel Productions) |
| Technique | Surface photogrammetry, height field generated by photogrammetry |
| Scanned size | ca. 2.1 m x 2.1 m, recorded as `tileMeters: 2.1` |

| Licence | [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/), published first-party by ambientCG |

Only the four 2K PNGs are used. The archive also holds a `.blend`, `.mtlx`, `.usdc`, `.tres`, a
1 MP thumbnail and the DirectX normal, which the cook never reads.

## Packing

The pair matches the rest of the library byte for byte in layout: 2048 x 2048, DXT5 (BC3)
FourCC, 12 mips (2048 to 1), 128-byte `DDS_HEADER`, mips largest first.

- `grass-color.dds`: RGB = albedo stored sRGB with every mip filtered in linear light; A = height
  from the 16-bit displacement (source range 0.29436-0.92709) normalised over its own range.
- `grass-surface.dds`: RGB = tangent normal from `NormalGL`, Green flipped for the top-down DDS
  rows, renormalised after each mip (mean 0.01494, 0.00005, 0.86989); A = roughness, linear.

Both files are the same size as the pair they replace (10.67 MiB together), so the landscape
payload figures on the Endor full landscape page do not move. Encode error against the intended
top level: colour 6.82/255 mean, 75 max; surface 11.56/255 mean, 156 max.

`grass.json` carries the row that replaces the `grass` entry of `materials.json`:

```json
{
  "id": "grass",
  "asset": "Ground037",
  "tileMeters": 2.1,
  "colorHash": "d630b84ff834fb208d55949b92a60cbb92329c07f464b85007b1fbdaf880900a",
  "surfaceHash": "00875729ea0be94f728c0b76ada9085949dfe462f5c0ae3fea51ffd9c6e7dd51"
}
```

The scan is 2.1 m across, so a tile left at the old 2.0 m repeat would crop the ground about 5%
larger. The row states the donor's own width; the other six rows are untouched.

## Before and after

Measured on the top level of the cooked albedo, i.e. exactly what the terrain samples. Green
dominance counts pixels with green more than 8 levels above red.

| | albedo mean (sRGB) | green / red | green - red | green-dominant |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Before, `leafy_grass` | 151.02, 131.34, 89.17 | 0.870 | -19.68 | 15.97% |
| After, `Ground037` | 152.44, 146.75, 87.57 | 0.963 | -5.69 | 20.03% |

The same 512-square crop of each 2048 tile (0.525 m after, 0.50 m before) is in
[`docs/endor-grass`](../../../docs/endor-grass): `before-crop.png`, `after-crop.png`, the mean
swatches, `means.json`, and `compare.html`, which wipes one over the other with a slider. Both
crops are straight from the pinned source PNGs; the sheets are regenerated by the cook with
`--evidence docs/endor-grass`.

Those are the whole set: a sweep of all 360 photogrammetry materials in the ambientCG catalogue
leaves `Ground037`, `Ground020`, `Ground030`, `Ground075` (a grass-reinforced parking grid) and

first-party Poly Haven grass scan is greener than this one. The procedural set is native 2K and
CC0 but painted by algorithm, so it was left out: the brief was a scan, and hand painting or
tinting the existing donor was not an option.

## Reproduce

```sh
node tools/cook-endor-grass.mjs                                  # cook, verify, write grass.json
node tools/cook-endor-grass.mjs --verify                         # + per-mip decode and encode error
node tools/cook-endor-grass.mjs --evidence docs/endor-grass      # + crops, means.json, compare.html
node tools/test-endor-grass.mjs                                  # offline check of the shipped pair
```

Inputs are fetched into `$ENDOR_GRASS_CACHE` or `<tmpdir>/endor-grass-source`, verified against
the pins above, and deleted once decoded; the repo keeps only the two DDS files and `grass.json`.
The cook uses the project's own `public/js/ddsmips.js` encoder and `public/js/ddsdecode.js`
decoder, so there are no dependencies to install, and the same inputs always give the same
sha256. `tools/test-endor-grass.mjs` re-reads both files from disk, checks hashes, headers,
dimensions, both 12-level chains, packed height and roughness, and that the albedo still beats
`leafy_grass` on green/red and green dominance. It needs no network.

Replacing the row and the file pair is the whole integration; `materials.json` and the
seven-family cook stay as they are, and `leafy_grass` is simply no longer that slot's donor.
