# Construction paving

The map paints seven natural families, but the engine can also paint a concrete construction layer when a building is placed. Do not let it silently use a stock tile in Full landscape mode. `paving.json` registers the eighth replacement: [Concrete Floor 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_02), by Rob Tuytel, [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license), measured width 2 m. `node tools/cook-forest-ground-04.mjs --paving --verify` downloads the hash-checked 2K diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness and height inputs from the [asset catalog](https://api.polyhaven.com/files/concrete_floor_02) and produces both complete BC3 chains. Input MD5s are in the cook script and output SHA-256s in `paving.json`. No donor or game content is fetched remotely by the player.
